# Finding Your Spotlight (FYS)

A high-performance personal brand platform and conversion-focused landing page built for **Jennis Williamson**, Purpose and Career Mentor for Creatives.

---

## 🌟 Overview

This project modernizes the online presence of *Finding Your Spotlight*, moving away from WordPress/Elementor to a fast, custom Next.js application. Built using a **Cinematic Noir** dark-mode aesthetic with high typographic contrast in **DM Sans**, the platform focuses purely on converting visitors into consultation clients and serving as an inline digital eBook storefront.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Conversion-Focused One-Pager:** Streamlined layout designed to guide visitors directly toward booking a 1-on-1 consultation.
* **Lean Intake Form:** Simple inquiry form capturing essential client details, session topics, and meeting preferences (Online vs. In-Person).
* **eBook Storefront:** Integrated checkout for digital eBook titles powered by Yoco with instant PDF delivery.
* **POPIA Compliant:** Embedded Privacy Policy and Terms of Service tailored for South African data legislation.
* **Admin Dashboard:** Private interface for managing booking requests, tracking eBook sales revenue, and updating book listings.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Framework:** Next.js (React / TypeScript)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, DM Sans Font
* **Database & Auth:** Supabase
* **Payments:** Yoco Gateway (ZAR)
* **Deployment:** Vercel
